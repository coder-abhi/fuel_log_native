## Full description (max 4000 chars)

```
Mileage and Fuel Tracker turns your fuel fill-ups into real numbers. Log each
refuel and the odometer reading that goes with it, and the app works out your
actual fuel mileage (km/l) over time — not the sticker figure, the one you're
really getting.

Everything runs on your phone. No account, no sign-up, no internet connection,
no ads, and no data ever leaves your device.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Track multiple vehicles, each with its own tank size and fuel history
• Log fill-ups in seconds: amount, cost, odometer, full or partial tank
• See your real mileage per fill-up and as a running average
• Estimate how much fuel is left in the tank and your remaining range
• Keep a service history (oil changes, tyre rotations, etc.) with distance-based
  reminders
• Review trends with clean, simple charts
• Log odometer readings on their own, between fill-ups

BUILT TO BE SIMPLE

The app is deliberately small. It does fuel and mileage tracking well and
doesn't try to be a full car-management suite. Data entry is quick, the numbers
update instantly, and past entries can be edited at any time — every calculation
recomputes automatically.

PRIVACY

Mileage and Fuel Tracker is fully offline. It collects nothing, sends nothing,
and shows no ads. Your log lives in a local database on your device and nowhere
else. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Note: fuel mileage is shown in kilometres per litre (km/l).
```
