Privacy Policy for Mileage and Fuel Tracker

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Mileage and Fuel Tracker ("the app") is an offline Android application that helps you record odometer readings, fuel fill-ups, and vehicle service history, and calculates fuel mileage and estimated range from that data. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or usage data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Data you enter

Everything you log — vehicles, odometer readings, fuel entries, service records, and app preferences — is stored only on your device in a local database. This data never leaves your phone. The developer has no access to it and no ability to retrieve it.

Network access

The app functions fully offline and does not make network requests. It does not send your data anywhere.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files, or any other sensitive device capability.

Data retention and deletion

Because all data is stored locally, you are in full control of it. You can delete individual entries within the app, or remove all data at once by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android Settings.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
